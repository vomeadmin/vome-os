---
name: reading-a-ticket-thread
description: Read a Zoho conversation thread in order, tell customer messages from internal replies, and find the most recent unanswered question.
owner: support
---

A thread is a sequence of messages with authors and timestamps. Read it in
time order even when it is presented newest first, because who said what last
decides what happens next.

**Tell the sides apart.** A message from the customer's own address is theirs.
A message from a vome address, or one signed Sam or Vic, is ours. A message
with no author that restates a status change is system noise, not a reply.

**Find the live question.** Work backwards from the newest message to the most
recent thing the customer asked that we have not answered. That question, not
the original subject line, is what the thread is currently about. A thread can
change topic three times, and answering the first question after the customer
has moved on reads as if nobody was listening.

**Auto-replies are not replies.** Out of office, delivery receipts, "this
mailbox is not monitored" and ticket-system acknowledgements carry no
information and must not be treated as the customer responding.

**A short reply is still a reply.** "Thanks", "got it", "perfect" close a loop.
They are not a new question and they are not a complaint.

**Watch for the same thing asked twice.** If the customer is repeating a
question we already answered, the previous answer did not land. Say so, and do
not simply send the same explanation again.

**Attachments carry weight.** A screenshot or an export usually contains the
detail the text left out. A thread with an attachment is never information
starved.

**Check the gap.** Note how long since the customer last wrote. A question from
this morning and a question from six weeks ago need different handling, and
"awaiting client" that has actually been abandoned is not the same as a
customer waiting on us.
